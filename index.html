01 · About me

For more than seven years, I've worked across product design, design systems, accessibility, research, and product strategy in complex SaaS environments.

I enjoy connecting disciplines that are often treated separately, turning scattered decisions into coherent systems that support users, empower teams, and create products that improve rather than deteriorate as they grow.

Good design, to me, isn't just clarity on the surface. It's stability underneath.

03 · My principles

Evidence-Driven Confidence

I test and iterate rigorously, because informed decisions come from evidence, not guesswork. That process is what lets me design experiences I actually trust.

Embracing Complexity

Hard problems energize me, especially the ones that demand real product thinking. I often work alongside others to turn messy, ambiguous challenges into something clear and aligned.

Collaborative Process

I build processes together with the people I work with, not around them, so everyone's bought in. And I keep that process loose enough to bend when priorities or circumstances shift.
